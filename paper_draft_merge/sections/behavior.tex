\section{Behavioral Phenomenon and Empirical Response Surfaces}
\label{sec:behavior}

\subsection{Outcomes and paired estimands}

The task, occurrence identity, response interfaces, and passage construction
are defined in \cref{sec:design}. Direct versus Native CoT is the primary
\emph{interface contrast}: in the frozen V2 suite it changes both the visible
response policy and the model's reasoning policy, so it is not interpreted as
a single-variable causal effect of CoT. Bullet and Index separately test an
explicit evidence ledger without unrestricted native reasoning.

The primary outcome is intent-to-treat numerical exactness of the parsed
integer. \emph{Exact-count accuracy} means that the final valid
\texttt{Total: <integer>} equals \(N\). \emph{Registered success} additionally
requires the mode-specific output format and no length truncation. We report
both and never call registered success ``accuracy'' without qualification.
Parse failure, strict format failure, truncation, protocol failure, trace
precision/recall, duplicate rate, and final-versus-enumerated consistency are
separate outcomes.

For model \(r\), mode \(m\), target count \(N\), and length \(L\), define
\begin{equation}
A_{rm}(N,L)=
\Pr(\widehat C=C\mid r,m,N,L),
\qquad
e=\widehat C-C,
\qquad
D_{rm}(N,L)=\E[|e|\mid r,m,N,L].
\label{eq:behavior-estimands}
\end{equation}
The paired interface contrast is
\begin{equation}
\Delta_r(N,L)=
A_{r,\mathrm{Native}}(N,L)-A_{r,\mathrm{Direct}}(N,L).
\label{eq:cot-gap}
\end{equation}
All mode effects are paired by frozen stimulus ID. Numerical exactness is
primary; signed bias and absolute error diagnose direction and magnitude.

For a trace, let \(\widehat G\) be the emitted multiset of aligned occurrence
IDs and let \(\widehat N\) be the parsed total. When both are available,
\begin{equation}
  \widehat N-N
  =
  \underbrace{\mathrm{FP}-\mathrm{FN}+\mathrm{Dup}}_
  {\text{retrieval and coverage}}
  +
  \underbrace{\left(\widehat N-|\widehat G|\right)}_
  {\text{aggregation or protocol}},
\label{eq:error-budget}
\end{equation}
where
\[
\mathrm{FP}=|\operatorname{uniq}(\widehat G)\setminus G_q|,
\quad
\mathrm{FN}=|G_q\setminus\operatorname{uniq}(\widehat G)|,
\quad
\mathrm{Dup}=|\widehat G|-|\operatorname{uniq}(\widehat G)|.
\]
This identity is applied only when the total and source identities are
parseable. Every other request remains an intent-to-treat parse, alignment,
truncation, or protocol failure rather than being dropped from a denominator.

\[
N\in\{1,2,3,4,5,6,8,10,20,30\},
\qquad
L\in\{2,3,5,10,20\}\text{k tokens},

Three empirical patterns motivate the mechanism study. First, all available trace-generating cells in \cref{tab:core-behavior} exceed their Direct counterpart. Second, CoT, Index, and Bullet occupy a similarly high band for the reliable Qwen, Gemma, and GLM cells; deviations such as Cogito Index and Nemotron Bullet show that trace format is not interchangeable for every model. Third, Direct is already accurate on some easy cells, which permits paired success/failure and non-ceiling mechanism analyses rather than comparing only a successful CoT state with a failed Direct state.

Across the full grid, accuracy generally decreases with larger \(N\) and \(L\), while \(|e|\) generally increases. The trend is not pointwise monotone for every model and mode. For example, parsing and truncation can distort Gemma4-E4B Direct length summaries, and the shared Direct response form performs poorly for Qwen3-8B. We therefore state the trend qualitatively until the held-out tests below are complete. The mechanism paper does not depend on a universal scaling law.

\subsection{Response surfaces are descriptive unless they predict held-out models}

We use one generic family of response surfaces for both accuracy and error:
\begin{align}
\operatorname{logit} A_{rm}(N,L)
  &=\alpha^{(A)}_{rm}
    +\beta^{(A)}_{N,rm}f(N)
    +\beta^{(A)}_{L,rm}g(L)
    +\beta^{(A)}_{NL,rm}f(N)g(L),\\
\log\!\left(1+D_{rm}(N,L)\right)
  &=\alpha^{(D)}_{rm}
    +\beta^{(D)}_{N,rm}f(N)
    +\beta^{(D)}_{L,rm}g(L)
    +\beta^{(D)}_{NL,rm}f(N)g(L).
\label{eq:response-surface}
\end{align}
Candidate bases \(f(N)\in\{N,\log N\}\) and \(g(L)\in\{L/1000,\log(L/1000)\}\), interactions, regularization, and held-condition splits are frozen before Qwen3-14B is generated. Coefficients are partially pooled within mode but remain model specific. A parametric form is promoted to an empirical law only if it predicts the held-out 14B results better than a no-size hierarchical baseline and remains stable under leave-one-size-out and family-held-out checks.

Current evidence does not meet that standard. The Native \(N\times L\) interaction is the most stable descriptive term across the eight development slots. Direct has useful average predictive structure but unstable coefficients and a negative cross-validated \(R^2\) for Qwen3-8B under the shared form. Index and Bullet have weak or unreliable shared surfaces. Accordingly, the main text will emphasize nonparametric \((N,L)\) heatmaps and partial contrasts; full coefficients and failed fits belong in the appendix.

\begin{todobox}

The main behavior figure should combine (a) Qwen size-panel predictions with the untouched 14B points and (b) Direct-versus-CoT cross-model partial-effect intervals. If validation succeeds, preregister a second extrapolation test with additional small Qwen checkpoints; if it fails, do not expand the size panel, replace ``law'' with qualitative response surfaces, and retain coefficients only as descriptive summaries.
\end{todobox}

\subsection{Behavior and mechanism panels remain separate}

The behavior panel is deliberately broad, whereas causal mechanism analysis
uses only Qwen3-8B and Gemma4-E4B on the controlled sibling corpus in
\cref{sec:design}. Native CoT is the ecological interface; unindexed
enumeration supplies the primary aligned trace for landmark discovery and
causal intervention; Index is the visible-label control. Canonical
teacher-forced traces measure local competence, while free-running traces
measure execution. Every emitted item is aligned to an occurrence or labeled
duplicate, invalid, hallucinated, or unresolved. Primary analyses include all
paired locked families; both-correct and error strata are diagnostics rather
than selection criteria.
