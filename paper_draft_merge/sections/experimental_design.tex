\section{Experimental Design}
\label{sec:design}

\subsection{Task and frozen naturalistic stimuli}

A passage contains source occurrences \(R_1,\ldots,R_M\). Under a model-specific
tokenizer, occurrence \(i\) occupies the half-open span \(I_i=[s_i,e_i)\). A
registered semantic predicate \(q\) gives
\begin{equation}
  y_i=\psi_q(R_i)\in\{0,1\},\qquad
  G_q=\{i:y_i=1\},\qquad N=|G_q|.
\label{eq:task}
\end{equation}
The source identity is the \emph{occurrence index} \(i\), not merely a city or
score string. Retrieving one occurrence twice is a duplicate; two distinct
valid occurrences both contribute to the count.

The behavioral suite is the frozen Realistic NIAH V2 grid. Filler text is
built from a content-deduplicated 218-source Paul Graham corpus. For each seed,
the source documents are shuffled once and the five length conditions use
nested prefixes; no essay is repeated to fill a context. We cross
\[
  L\in\{2000,3000,5000,10000,20000\},\qquad
  N\in\{1,2,3,4,5,6,8,10,20,30\},
\]
with seeds \(1234,\ldots,1243\), yielding 500 frozen base passages. \(L\) is
the exact post-insertion length under the frozen Qwen3-8B tokenizer; realized
length under every evaluated tokenizer is also stored.

Every valid record has the literal form
\begin{quote}\small
\texttt{In the 2024 city score audit, <city> received a score of <score>.}
\end{quote}
Cities and scores are sampled without replacement within a passage. Candidate
insertion sites are conservative sentence endings that exclude numeric
strings, URLs, file paths, and common abbreviation periods. Sites are sampled
without replacement when possible; any replacement or word-boundary fallback
is logged. The generator changes only neutral filler and regenerates until the
\emph{post-insertion} canonical length is exact. A stimulus is rejected unless
every target occurs exactly once, no target-like sentence occurs in the
filler, every character span maps to a verified token span, and no insertion
is truncated. Passage bytes, occurrence identities, insertion depths, and a
SHA256 digest are frozen before expanding modes or models. Thus all modes and
models for one behavioral stimulus receive the identical passage text.

\subsection{Controlled sibling stimuli for mechanism identification}

The naturalistic grid measures behavior but cannot by itself distinguish a
record-wise counter from late aggregation: increasing \(N\) also inserts more
record-like sentences. We therefore construct a disjoint, tokenizer-audited
mechanism corpus. A stimulus family
\begin{equation}
  \omega=(H,q,\{u_j,I_j,y_j\}_{j=1}^{K})
\label{eq:family}
\end{equation}
contains the same filler \(H\), \(K=10\) fixed record-like slots, opaque
occurrence IDs \(u_j\), and positive/negative variants that differ in exactly
one predicate-critical attribute. Hard negatives use a wrong audit type, such
as \texttt{city safety audit}, or a wrong document type, such as
\texttt{city score report}. Year alone is never used as a negative because
the frozen V2 predicate does not restrict year.

For each mechanism model, rejection sampling makes every positive/negative
pair equal in token length. Slots are drawn once from ten normalized-depth
strata at legal sentence boundaries, with a target separation of at least 128
model tokens. Slot text, order, and token positions remain fixed across
siblings at one length. A balanced permutation chooses which \(N\) slots are
positive, so validity is not predicted by depth, negative type, city, or
score. Qwen and Gemma corpora are rendered separately to make post-insertion
length exact under each tokenizer. They share the semantic family manifest,
slot order, labels, and normalized depths, but are not assumed to share token
IDs or absolute token indices.

We register two paired manipulations:
\begin{itemize}
  \item A \emph{prefix-shift} pair exchanges an early positive slot \(r\) and
  a late matched negative slot \(s>r\), preserving final \(N\), \(L\), slot
  identities, and all downstream token indices while changing the cumulative
  count only for \(r\leq j<s\).
  \item A \emph{total-shift} pair flips one equal-length slot from negative to
  positive, producing \(N\leftrightarrow N+1\) without changing its location
  or the length of any suffix.
\end{itemize}
Every clean/corrupt pair stores complete prompt and continuation token IDs,
modified character/token spans, and length-alignment checks.

\begin{todobox}
\textbf{D1---freeze the controlled mechanism corpus.}
Use \(N\in\{0,\ldots,6,8,10\}\). At the primary length \(L=5\)k, assign at
least 120 independent families before activation capture: 60 discovery, 20
validation, and 40 locked test families. A family-cluster pilot power analysis
may increase, but may not silently shrink, the locked set. After layers,
heads, thresholds, and intervention doses are frozen, evaluate at least 40 new
locked families at each of \(L=2\)k and \(L=10\)k. Prefix shift is eligible
only for \(1\leq N\leq K-1\); at \(N=0\) or \(N=K\), use the available
one-direction total shift. Use data seeds disjoint from the behavioral grid
and publish the semantic manifest, tokenized realizations, contamination
audit, and all rejected generations.
\end{todobox}

\subsection{Prompt serialization, reasoning policy, and decoding}

The frozen behavioral layout is \emph{predicate-before, suffix-after}. A common
cue and the definition of a city-score audit record precede
\texttt{<passage>}; the response request and mode-specific policy follow
\texttt{</passage>}. The passage and final question are reused byte for byte
across modes. The response policy and, where available, the model's reasoning
switch define the observable interface:

Because the mode-specific suffix follows the passage, a causal transformer
cannot make passage-token states mode specific when the rendered prefix
through the passage is identical. We verify this condition after applying
each checkpoint's exact chat template. Passing passage states are treated as
a \emph{shared scanning substrate}, not as evidence that CoT constructed a
cleaner representation while reading. Mode-specific passage encoding is
studied only in a separate \emph{policy-before} control that moves the
Direct/enumeration instruction before the passage; those states are never
pooled with the frozen behavioral grid.

The mechanism study additionally crosses visible instruction (Direct versus
unindexed enumeration) with the Qwen/Gemma reasoning switch (off versus on),
forming a \(2\times2\) instruction-by-policy factorial. Predicate placement
has three levels: predicate-before, \emph{predicate-after}, and
\emph{predicate-repeat}. Predicate-after record states cannot encode the
task-specific cumulative count and provide a causal-mask negative control;
predicate-repeat distinguishes availability during encoding from instruction
recency.

Data-generation and decoding seeds are separate fields. The ten V2 seeds are
stimulus replicates, not repeated samples from one decoding distribution. For
stochastic endpoints, clean, corrupt, and patched rollouts use common random
numbers and at least three paired decoding seeds. Parse failure, format
failure, and truncation remain in intent-to-treat summaries. We report
teacher-forced likelihood of the \emph{complete registered continuation} and
free-generation exact count and MAE; a first-digit logit is never the sole
endpoint. Equal-length null scratchpads control for extra tokens and recurrent
computation.

\begin{todobox}
\textbf{D2---prompt and policy controls.}
Run the \(2\times2\) instruction-by-policy factorial, the three predicate
placements, and policy-before versus suffix-after on locked Qwen3-8B and
Gemma4-E4B mechanism families. Freeze checkpoint and tokenizer revisions, chat
templates, stop rules, generation budgets, and parsers. Hash every rendered
prefix through the final passage token. Use the same sampler, maximum budget,
and paired generation seeds within a reasoning-toggle contrast. Any claimed
algorithmic change must survive controls for instruction recency, output
length, null computation, and checkpoints that reason in every nominal mode.
\end{todobox}

\subsection{Registered token landmarks and activation capture}

For block \(\ell\), \(h_t^\ell\) is the \texttt{resid\_post} stream returned
after that block's residual additions and before block \(\ell+1\) consumes the
state. Embedding states are diagnostic only. Every token index is obtained
from character-to-token offsets on the \emph{final model-specific
chat-rendered sequence}; no index is inferred by adding the length of a
standalone needle.

Controlled records end with the invariant delimiter
\[
  D_{\rm eor}=\texttt{[END-RECORD]}.
\]
Writing \(a_i\) for the token overlapping its closing bracket, the registered
Direct record state is \(H_i^\ell=h_{a_i}^\ell\). The Direct terminal state
\(Z_D^\ell=h_{q_D}^\ell\) uses the token overlapping the colon in a
teacher-forced \texttt{Total:} prefix, immediately before the first count
token. In controlled CoT, the unindexed grammar is
\[
  \texttt{Item: <city>: <score> [END-RECORD]}.
\]
The retrieval state \(Q_k^\ell=h_{q_k^{\rm ret}}^\ell\) is extracted at the
colon in \texttt{Item:}, before source-bearing content. The progress state
\(P_k^\ell=h_{q_k^{\rm end}}^\ell\) is extracted at the closing bracket after
the \(k\)th source mention and before any next index or label. The controlled
terminal \(Z_C^{\rm ctrl,\ell}\) is the \texttt{Total:} colon after the
canonical teacher-forced trace. A uniquely aligned \texttt{Total:} colon in a
free-running native trace defines \(Z_C^{\rm native,\ell}\); native and
controlled terminals are never pooled.

The chat prompt is tokenized with the exact generation-time template. A
teacher-forced continuation is appended as continuation token IDs with no
additional special tokens; we never retokenize the concatenated raw string in
a way that can alter the final prompt token. A sample is rejected if the token
overlapping \texttt{Item:} also overlaps the first source character or if the
token overlapping \texttt{Total:} contains any count character. The manifest
stores raw messages, rendered text, prompt and continuation token IDs,
character spans, token spans, landmark indices, model revision, tokenizer
revision, and hook name.

At a Direct record endpoint, the semantic label is
\begin{equation}
  c_q(a_i)=\sum_{j\leq i}y_j.
\label{eq:prefix-count}
\end{equation}
For a CoT trace, align emitted step \(k\) to
\(\pi_k\in\{1,\ldots,M\}\cup\{\bot\}\), where \(\bot\) denotes an unaligned
item. Define
\begin{equation}
  \mathcal V_k=\operatorname{uniq}
  \{\pi_u:1\leq u\leq k,\ \pi_u\neq\bot\},
  \qquad
  p_k=|\mathcal V_k\cap G_q|.
\label{eq:semantic-progress}
\end{equation}
Thus CoT progress is the number of distinct valid occurrences actually
retrieved, not the visible step number.

\subsection{Analysis unit, splitting, and averaging}

The atomic representation observation is
\[
  \text{one family}\times\text{one rendered variant}\times
  \text{one registered landmark}\times\text{one layer}.
\]
The stimulus family is the independent unit for splitting and uncertainty.
All siblings, modes, predicate placements, \((N,L)\) variants, and
clean/corrupt counterfactuals remain in the same family split. Discovery fits
preprocessing and selects layers/heads; validation freezes hyperparameters,
thresholds, and intervention doses; locked test families are opened once.
Additional transfer tests hold out record lexemes, templates, and complete
\((N,L)\) cells.

Let \(\mathcal B\) be families, \(\mathcal V_b\) the eligible rendered variants
of family \(b\), and \(J_{bv}\) the eligible landmarks in variant \(v\).
Representation objectives use
\begin{equation}
  \frac{1}{|\mathcal B|}\sum_{b\in\mathcal B}
  \frac{1}{|\mathcal V_b|}\sum_{v\in\mathcal V_b}
  \frac{1}{|J_{bv}|}\sum_{j\in J_{bv}}
  \mathcal L\!\left(g(h_{bvj}^\ell),y_{bvj}\right).
\label{eq:family-weighting}
\end{equation}
No hidden state is averaged across prompts before PCA, probing, or primary
patching. PCA centering, nuisance regression, decoder regularization, and
directions are fitted on discovery observations only. Figures may show
individual locked-test points and family centroids, but centroids are
summaries rather than substituted observations.

Primary natural-state patching uses one matched donor. A discovery-set mean
state is only an ablation control. Geometric steering is the sole causal
intervention whose direction is defined by a cross-prompt mean, estimated from
paired discovery-family differences. Attention statistics are averaged first
over eligible steps within a variant, then over variants within a family, and
finally equally over families. Raw hidden vectors are never averaged across
layers, checkpoints, token roles, or model families.
